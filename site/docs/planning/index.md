# Product Requirements Document

**Contactless Order System** · Version 1.0 · March 2026  
Status: **Draft — For Client Review**

---

## 1. Executive Summary

The **Contactless Order System** is a SaaS platform that enables restaurants to offer a fully digital, app-free ordering experience. Guests scan a QR code at their table, browse a live digital menu, and place orders directly from their personal device. Orders flow in real time to kitchen staff, who update statuses visible to both guests and front-of-house staff. Restaurant owners manage menus, tables, and reporting through a dedicated dashboard.

The platform is designed for rapid onboarding of multiple restaurants under a single multi-tenant architecture, with strong role separation and real-time communication as core design principles.

---

## 2. Problem Statement

Traditional restaurant ordering suffers from:

- **Staff bottlenecks** — Guests wait for staff to take orders manually, causing delays during peak hours.
- **Error-prone hand-off** — Verbal or handwritten orders are misheard or missed.
- **No guest visibility** — Guests have no insight into order status; they must flag down staff for updates.
- **High printing costs** — Paper menus must be reprinted for every menu change.
- **COVID-era hygiene** — Physical menus and shared surfaces remain a contact risk concern for health-conscious diners.

## 4. Scope

### In Scope (v1.0)

- QR code generation per restaurant (one HMAC-signed QR per restaurant; guest enters table number at checkout)
- Guest-facing digital menu browse & order
- Real-time order status tracking (guest + kitchen + staff)
- Thermal print ticket routing (kitchen printer + bar printer)
- Staff management dashboard
- Restaurant owner menu & table management
- Multi-restaurant platform admin panel
- Supabase-backed auth (JWT) and data persistence
- SignalR WebSocket hub for live state sync
- Mobile-first responsive UI (no native app required)
- PWA (installable web app — Service Worker + Web App Manifest)

### Out of Scope (v1.0)

- Payment processing / in-app checkout
- Loyalty programs or promo codes
- Inventory management or stock alerts
- Table reservation / booking system
- Native iOS/Android apps
- Third-party POS integrations (planned v2)
- Multi-language menu content (planned v2)

## 5. User Personas & Roles

### 5.1 Guest (Diner)

> A customer seated at a restaurant table with a smartphone.

- Has **no account** — accesses the system by scanning a QR code
- Wants to browse the menu, customise items, and place an order
- Wants visibility on order status without flagging down staff
- May place multiple orders during a single visit

### 5.2 Staff (Waiter / Front-of-House)

> A restaurant employee who manages tables and assists guests.

- Authenticates via **PIN** (no password setup required)
- Can view all active tables and orders
- Can mark orders as served / close a table session
- Can override or cancel guest orders

### 5.3 Restaurant Owner / Manager

> The business owner or manager of a single restaurant.

- Full account with **Google OAuth** sign-in — no password setup required
- Account starts in **Pending Approval** status; must be activated by PlatformAdmin after fee payment is confirmed
- Manages the digital menu (add/edit/archive items, set availability)
- Manages tables (add/remove, generate/refresh QR codes)
- Views order history, revenue reports, and guest feedback
- Manages staff PINs and accounts

### 5.4 Platform Administrator

> The operator of the SaaS platform — has access to all tenants.

- Creates and manages restaurant tenants
- Views cross-tenant metrics and health
- Can suspend or deactivate restaurants
- Reviews and **approves** new restaurant owner registrations after fee payment is confirmed
- Manages platform-wide configuration

### F3 — Real-Time Order Tracking

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|----|-------------|----------|
| F3.1 | New orders trigger a print event via SignalR — food items routed to kitchen printer, drink items to bar printer | Must Have |
| F3.2 | Order status auto-advances to "Preparing" when the print ticket is dispatched | Must Have |
| F3.3 | Guest sees live order status updates on their device without page refresh | Must Have |
| F3.4 | Status change triggers an on-screen notification to the guest | Should Have |
| F3.5 | Staff dashboard shows all active orders across all tables, sortable by time | Must Have |
| F3.6 | System alerts if an order has been in "Received" state > 5 minutes (configurable) | Could Have |

### F4 — Thermal Print System

| ID | Requirement | Priority |
|----|-------------|----------|
| F4.1 | Each menu item is tagged as **food** or **drink** to determine print routing | Must Have |
| F4.2 | Food items in an order are automatically printed on the **kitchen thermal printer** | Must Have |
| F4.3 | Drink items in an order are automatically printed on the **bar thermal printer** | Must Have |
| F4.4 | Each printed ticket shows: table number, order number, items, quantities, and item notes | Must Have |
| F4.5 | Kitchen and bar printers operate independently — a drink-only order does not trigger the kitchen printer | Must Have |
| F4.6 | Each restaurant registers two **Star mC-Print3** printers (kitchen + bar) in the Owner Dashboard. Printers use **Star CloudPRNT**: they poll `GET /api/print/poll?deviceToken=<token>` every 2–3 s; the backend responds with Base64-encoded Star ESC/POS commands. No local software is installed at the restaurant. | Must Have |
| F4.7 | Print failure (printer offline or job unconsumed after timeout) surfaces a visual alert in the Staff Dashboard | Should Have |

## 9. Data Model (High-Level)

```
Restaurant (owner_status: pending|active|suspended, qr_token, kitchen_device_token, bar_device_token)
  ├── Tables (table_number)
  ├── MenuCategories (name, sort_order)
  │   └── MenuItems (name, description, price_eur_cents, photo_url, is_available, item_type: food|drink)
  │         └── MenuItemModifierGroups (name, is_required, min_selections, max_selections)
  │               └── MenuItemModifiers (name, price_delta_eur_cents)
  ├── Staff (name, pin_hash, role)
  └── Orders
        ├── (restaurant_id, table_number, session_id, status, created_at)
        └── OrderItems (menu_item_id, quantity, note, status)
              └── OrderItemModifiers (modifier_id, name_snapshot, price_delta_cents)
```

## 10. User Journey: Happy Path

```
1. Guest sits at table → scans the restaurant QR code
2. Browser opens menu page (no install, no login)
3. Guest browses categories, adds items, selects any required modifiers, optionally adds notes
4. Guest selects their table number at checkout and taps "Place Order" → order submitted
5. Food items auto-print on the kitchen thermal printer; drink items auto-print on the bar thermal printer
6. Order status advances to "Preparing" automatically
7. Guest sees ⟳ "Being prepared" on their device
8. Kitchen / bar staff prepare items from the printed ticket — no screen interaction needed
9. Staff delivers food and drinks → marks "Served" in the Staff Dashboard
10. Guest sees ✓ "Order complete"
11. Guest may place additional orders (repeat from step 3)
12. Guest pays at the restaurant counter → Staff closes the table session
```
